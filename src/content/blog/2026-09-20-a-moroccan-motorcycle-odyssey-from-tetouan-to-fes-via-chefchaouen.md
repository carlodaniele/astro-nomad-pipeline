# A Moroccan Motorcycle Odyssey: From Tetouan to Fes via Chefchaouen

On April 17th, an intrepid motorcycle journey commenced from Tetouan, destined for Fes, with a significant stopover in the renowned blue city of Chefchaouen. This adventure proved to be both geographically diverse and remarkably challenging from its outset, demanding constant attention from the riders.

## The Challenging Route Through the Rif

The road conditions throughout the journey were far from ideal. The route was often tortuous and in a state described as truly disastrous and very uneven. It was not a simple commute but a true transit through deep potholes, sudden interruptions, and even landslides. Adding to the complexity was heavy truck traffic, which made every overtaking maneuver a perilous undertaking.

## Chefchaouen: The Blue Pearl of Morocco

Geographically, the route traversed the foothills of the Rif Mountains. Chefchaouen, often called the "Blue City," presented a striking visual experience. Its streets are famously painted in various shades of blue, creating an ethereal atmosphere. Staircases adorned with vibrant pom-poms and colorful potted plants, as seen in its alleys, contribute to its unique charm. Founded in 1471, Chefchaouen served as a fortress against Portuguese incursions and later became a refuge for Moriscos and Jews fleeing the Reconquista, influencing its distinct architecture and cultural heritage.

## Shifting Landscapes Southward

As the journey continued southward from Chefchaouen, the landscape underwent a noticeable metamorphosis. The rugged mountains gradually gave way to a succession of cultivated hills. Here, fields of grain were already approaching maturation, signaling a shift in the agricultural scenery of the region.

## Fes: A Labyrinthine Ancient City

The day concluded upon arrival in Fes, where the ancient medina awaited. Fes, specifically Fes el-Bali, is one of the world's largest car-free urban areas and a UNESCO World Heritage site. Founded in the 8th century, it is considered the spiritual and cultural capital of Morocco, boasting the oldest university in the world. The medina of Fes is an impressive and intricate labyrinth, where navigating without a guide can be exceptionally difficult. The dense network of alleyways, souks, and historic buildings immerses visitors in a truly ancient world. The initial encounter with this old city represented the culmination of the day's experiences, leaving a profound sense of wonder at everything witnessed.
